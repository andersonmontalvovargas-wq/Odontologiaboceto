Smile makeover

Porcelain veneers

Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made shells of high-quality ceramic, bonded to the front of the teeth to correct discoloration, chips, gaps and minor misalignment. We work with E.max and Empress porcelain.

  • 3D digital smile design, so you can preview your smile before we start.
  • Full digital workflow: 3D scanning, 3D printing and CAD/CAM.
  • A full makeover usually includes 16 to 20 veneers (upper and lower arches).
  • Treatment typically takes 5 to 7 business days.

The cost of your treatment is defined during your consultation, which can start virtually.

Common questions

How much do porcelain veneers cost?
